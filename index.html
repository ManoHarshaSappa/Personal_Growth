Hair Care & Styling
Face Ratio Analysis: Your face ratio is 1.5-1.7 (slightly longer than wide). Your thick hair is one of your strongest features - keep volume on top!

✅ Recommended Haircuts (Perfect for Your Face Shape):

  • Textured Quiff: Push volume forward and up - ideal choice
  • Low Taper Fade + Curly Top: Maintain natural curl with tight sides
  • Messy Textured Crop: Natural texture with controlled styling
  • Side Part + Taper Fade: Classic professional look
  • Medium-Length Wavy Flow: Let natural texture shine

❌ Avoid These Cuts:

  • Buzz Cut: Will make face appear wider
  • High Skin Fade with Very Short Top: Removes your best feature
  • Completely Shaved Head: Emphasizes round face shape

Weekly Hair Care Routine:

  • Wash Days: Every 3-4 days (Monday/Thursday)
  • Conditioner: Every wash, focus on mid-length to ends
  • Hair Mask: Weekly (Sunday nights) - moisturizing mask for curly hair
  • Dry Shampoo: Between wash days if needed

Daily Styling Routine:

  • Apply leave-in curl cream to damp hair
  • Use sea salt spray for texture definition
  • Scrunch gently, air dry or diffuse on low heat
  • Avoid heavy gels that flatten natural texture

Haircut Schedule:

  • Professional Cut: Every 4-6 weeks for textured styles
  • Fade Touch-up: Every 2-3 weeks for taper fades
  • Ask for: "Textured quiff with low fade, keep natural curl and volume on top"

Frizz Control & Drying:

  • Never brush when dry - only when wet with conditioner
  • Use microfiber towel or cotton t-shirt to dry
  • Sleep on silk pillowcase to reduce frizz
  • Key: Embrace your thick texture - it's your strongest feature!

Beard Grooming
Target: 6-10mm shaped beard with sharp lines

Daily Routine:

  • Beard oil: 3-4 drops after shower, massage into skin
  • Brushing: Downward strokes with boar bristle brush
  • Direction: Train hair to grow downward from jaw

Weekly Maintenance:

  • Trimming: Use 6-8mm guard for even length
  • Neckline: Two-finger rule above Adam's apple
  • Cheek line: Clean, defined line from sideburn to mustache corner
  • Mustache: Trim above lip line, connect naturally

Neckline Definition (Critical!):

  • Place two fingers above Adam's apple
  • Create clean line from ear to ear above this point
  • Remove ALL hair below this line
  • Use precision trimmer for sharp edges

Products Needed:

  • Quality beard trimmer with adjustable guards
  • Beard oil (jojoba-based)
  • Boar bristle brush
  • Precision trimmer for edges
