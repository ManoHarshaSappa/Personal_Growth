# 📸 Images Folder

Add your personal photos here with these exact names:

## Required Images:

- **`hair-front.jpg`** - Front view of your hair
- **`hair-side.jpg`** - Side view of your hair  
- **`hair-back.jpg`** - Back view of your hair
- **`beard.jpg`** - Close-up view of your beard

## Image Guidelines:

- **Format:** JPG, JPEG, PNG
- **Size:** Recommended 400x400px to 800x800px
- **Quality:** Good lighting, clear photos
- **Naming:** Exact names as listed above (case-sensitive)

## How to Add:

1. Take or select your photos
2. Rename them exactly as shown above
3. Place them in this `images` folder
4. Refresh the webpage to see them display

The app will automatically display your photos once they're added with the correct names.
