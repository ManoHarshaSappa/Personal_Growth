### Best haircut styles for round-oval face + 2B/2C texture
1. **Textured curly crop** — keep natural curl on top, tight fade on sides. Adds height, defines curls.
2. **Modern quiff / wavy quiff** — push volume forward and up. Works beautifully with your wave pattern.
3. **Mid-fade with volume on top** — sides faded to skin or low guard, top left curly and full.
4. **Edgar cut (curly)** — blunt fringe version, very trendy. Works well with high-volume curly hair.

> **Product tip:** A light curl cream or sea salt spray will define your 2B/2C waves without weighing them down. Avoid heavy gels that flatten the natural texture.
